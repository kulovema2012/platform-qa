# Online store

| Role | Feature | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Shopper | Product search, filters and sorting | Shoppers who cannot find a product do not buy it |
| Shopper | Clear stock status and delivery estimate | Surprises after payment cause cancellations |
| Shopper | Cart that survives sign-in and returns later | Abandoned carts are lost revenue |
| Shopper | Guest checkout | Forced sign-up loses first-time buyers |
| Shopper | Order history and status tracking | Reduces "where is my order?" contacts |
| Shopper | Cancel or change an order before it ships | Common request; manual handling is costly |
| Shopper | Returns and refund request | Expected by buyers and required by consumer law in many regions |
| Shopper | Saved addresses and payment methods | Faster repeat purchases |
| Shopper | Receipts and tax invoices | Business buyers need them |
| Shopper | Discount codes with clear rules | Confusing promotions cause complaints |
| Staff | Order management: fulfil, partly ship, cancel, refund | Daily operations |
| Staff | Inventory management with low-stock alerts | Overselling and stockouts |
| Staff | Product management with variants, images, prices | Catalogue upkeep |
| Staff | Customer lookup with order history | Support needs context |
| Staff | Refund and return processing with reasons | Money control and fraud spotting |
| Admin | Promotions with limits (per customer, dates, minimums) | Uncontrolled discounts lose money |
| Admin | Shipping zones, rates and tax settings | Correct pricing per region |
| Admin | Sales reports by period, product and channel | Business decisions |
| Admin | Fraud signals (many failed payments, unusual orders) | Chargebacks are expensive |
| Owner | Payment provider settings and payouts view | Money reconciliation |

This catalogue is a starting point. Check the app's own docs and decisions before calling anything missing.
