# Booking and scheduling

| Role | Feature | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | See real availability in their own time zone | Wrong times cause no-shows |
| Customer | Book without double-booking a slot | Two customers for one slot damages trust |
| Customer | Confirmation with calendar invite | People forget bookings they cannot see |
| Customer | Reminders before the booking | Cuts no-shows significantly |
| Customer | Reschedule | Cheaper than cancel plus rebook; keeps the customer |
| Customer | Cancel with clear policy and refund rules | Disputes otherwise |
| Customer | Waitlist for full slots | Fills cancellations automatically |
| Customer | Booking history | Repeat bookings and receipts |
| Staff | Own schedule, working hours, breaks and time off | Bookings during unavailable time |
| Staff | Day and week views of bookings | Daily operations |
| Staff | Mark no-show and attendance | Fair policies and reporting |
| Staff | Block time and create bookings for walk-in or phone customers | Not every booking comes through the website |
| Admin | Services with duration, price, buffer time and capacity | Realistic scheduling |
| Admin | Resources (rooms, equipment) and capacity limits | Overbooking shared resources |
| Admin | Cancellation and late-change policy settings | Enforced consistently |
| Admin | Deposits or prepayment for bookings | Reduces no-shows for costly slots |
| Admin | Reports: utilization, no-show rate, revenue | Staffing and pricing decisions |
| Admin | Holiday and closure calendar | Prevents bookings on closed days |
| Owner | Multi-location settings | Growth beyond one site |

This catalogue is a starting point. Check the app's own docs and decisions before calling anything missing.
